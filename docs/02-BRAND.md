# NOVIN — Brand DNA

## Brand Idea
**Better everyday choices, presented with warmth and confidence.**

NOVIN should feel curated rather than crowded, premium rather than flashy, and human rather than sterile.

## Personality
- Warm
- Tactile
- Refined
- Trustworthy
- Contemporary
- Calm
- Useful

## Art Direction
**Tactile Warm Commerce**

Visual references should lean toward editorial product styling, warm natural materials, quiet premium retail and restrained micro-interaction rather than glass-heavy startup UI or generic marketplace aesthetics.

## Color System — Direction
### Core
- Ivory Cream — `#F7F2EA`
- Espresso — `#3B2F28`
- Warm Taupe — `#A88F78`
- Clay Copper — `#C7845C`
- Soft Sage — `#8D9B87`

### Usage Principles
- Ivory is the primary canvas, not pure white everywhere.
- Espresso provides readable text and premium depth.
- Copper is an intentional CTA/accent color, not a decorative flood.
- Sage is supporting and should not compete with commerce CTAs.
- Product imagery may introduce controlled category colors.

Final accessible token values must be validated for contrast before implementation.

## Logotype Direction
NOVIN needs a custom typographic identity rather than a font-size treatment.

Required identity set:
- Primary NOVIN wordmark
- Compact wordmark
- `N` monogram
- Favicon/app icon
- Light/dark/mono variants
- Motion-safe animated logo treatment for splash/campaign use

### Letterform Character
- Soft geometric construction
- Subtle organic curves
- Crafted terminals
- Strong custom kerning
- Memorable `N` and/or `O` construction usable as brand motifs
- Premium, approachable, not fashion-cold and not playful-kids retail

## Graphic Language
The brand system should derive shapes from the logo geometry. Potential motifs:
- softened arches
- nested product frames
- tactile paper/ceramic-inspired surfaces
- quiet contour lines
- crop-window framing for product imagery

Avoid using random gradients, generic bento layouts or repeated AI-template motifs as primary identity devices.

## Typography Direction
### Persian
Use a modern Persian typeface with excellent UI readability, strong numeric forms and compatibility with commerce/data surfaces.

### English
Pair with a contemporary humanist or refined grotesk family rather than a futuristic SaaS face.

Typography selection must be tested together across:
- navigation
- price
- product titles
- long descriptions
- admin tables
- forms
- mobile UI

## UI Character
- Warm neutral surfaces
- Product-first hierarchy
- Controlled rounded geometry
- Tactile micro-interactions
- Minimal visual noise
- Strong mobile bottom-action patterns where useful
- Drawers/sheets that feel integrated with the brand

## Motion Personality
- Fast but calm
- Subtle tactile feedback
- Small depth and scale changes
- No constant floating/glowing effects
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Photography Direction
- Warm directional natural light
- Neutral architectural/still-life backgrounds
- Controlled shadows
- Material detail
- Product-centric negative space
- Consistent crop language

## Brand Image Prompt
Create a premium warm lifestyle e-commerce image for a bilingual Persian/English brand named NOVIN. Show a carefully styled selection of contemporary lifestyle, digital, home and accessory products arranged with refined editorial composition on warm ivory and natural material surfaces. Use soft directional daylight, tactile textures, subtle realistic shadows, premium but approachable styling, restrained earthy accents, and generous negative space for website typography. Avoid generic stock-photo aesthetics, excessive luxury gloss, neon colors, glassmorphism, visible logos, and clutter.

## Anti-Repetition Rule
NOVIN's brand DNA, component geometry, image treatment and motion language must not be reused as the final visual identity of template-2 through template-10.
